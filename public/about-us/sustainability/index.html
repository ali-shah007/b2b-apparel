LET'S TALK

Ethical & Sustainable

Fashioning a
Better Future.

We believe that exceptional clothing shouldn't come at the cost of our planet or its people. Here's how TA Sports weaves sustainability and ethics into every project.

Considered Sourcing

Eco-Friendly
Materials

We help brands make environmentally conscious choices by sourcing lower-impact materials and giving priority to suppliers with responsible production practices.

Recycled Polyester & Nylon

Where available, sourced from post-consumer recycled inputs to help reduce landfill waste.

Organic & Low-Impact Cotton

We offer organic cotton options for brands looking to reduce their environmental footprint.

Cruelty-Free Commitment

We work with our partners to avoid real fur, exotic leathers, and angora in our manufacturing.

Ethical Workspaces

People First.

Beautiful garments require happy, healthy teams. We take pride in prioritizing safe, supportive, and fair working environments across our manufacturing partnerships.

Fair Wages

We prioritize partners who compensate workers fairly for their labor.

Safe Conditions

Modern, well-ventilated facilities that put worker safety first.

Zero Discrimination

Equal opportunity workspaces that welcome every background.

Ongoing Accountability

We continually evaluate partners against our ethical standards.

Recognized Standards

Certified
Integrity

Transparency matters to real sustainability. We prioritize working with fabric mills and suppliers who pursue globally recognized standards, and we ask them to back that up with documentation.

GRS Global Recycled Standard
GOTS Global Organic Textile Standard
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Certified
ISO Quality & Environmental
